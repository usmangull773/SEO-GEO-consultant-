01 What the review surfaced

Full list, most urgent first. Severity shows what to settle before the rebuild.

AI search & GEO visibility
Critical

AI engines list competitors as the leaders, not FLEXECHARGE

For your core category, AI search currently names AMPECO, ChargePoint, Monta and Virta Hub. FLEXECHARGE is not mentioned, even though it ranks #1 in standard Google results.

Fix  Build entity clarity, citable answer-ready pages and consistent brand signals so AI models surface you for your own category.

Good

Your robots.txt already welcomes AI crawlers

GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot are explicitly allowed. A solid base to build the GEO layer on.

Medium

No llms.txt yet

There is no llms.txt to point AI crawlers at your most important pages, an easy early-mover win as the new structure ships.

Fix  Add an llms.txt aligned to the new pain-point structure.

Indexation & migration risk
Critical

Far more URLs are indexed than your sitemap lists

Google indexes well over a thousand URLs for the site, while your sitemap lists only 74. Redirecting just the sitemap URLs during the rebuild would strand the rest, including pages that hold rankings and AI citations.

Fix  A 1:1 redirect map for every live URL, not only the sitemap, before switching to pain-point pages.

Host, canonical & redirects
High

Apex-to-www works, but through a 2-hop redirect chain

The homepage resolves correctly to https + www, but via two 301 redirects instead of one, which wastes crawl signal.

Fix  Collapse to a single 301 hop to the final URL.

Medium

A separate subdomain is also indexed

A video subdomain appears in the index alongside the main site, with no clear host policy tying them together.

Fix  Set one canonical host and subdomain policy across the whole property.

On-page, headings & schema
High

Two Organization schemas, with office addresses that disagree with your footer

The homepage carries two competing Organization entities, and the addresses inside them do not match the addresses in your own footer. That confuses both Google and AI about who you are.

Fix  One authoritative Organization entity, with a consistent name and address everywhere.

High

Two H1 headings on the homepage

The homepage has two H1 tags (one hidden in the code), which blurs the page's main-topic signal.

Fix  One clear H1 per page, with a logical heading order in the new templates.

Medium

FAQ-style sections with no FAQ schema

Product pages use accordion Q&A blocks but expose no FAQ, Service or Breadcrumb structured data, missing straightforward AI and rich-result wins.

Fix  Bake FAQ, Service and Breadcrumb schema into the solution-page template.

Performance & the new map
High

The homepage carries a heavy load of third-party scripts

A large number of third-party scripts weigh down how fast the homepage becomes usable. The new interactive map will add to that if its performance is not budgeted up front.

Fix  Trim and defer non-critical scripts, and set a performance budget for the map before launch.

Medium

The interactive Europe map must stay crawlable and accessible

If the new map renders its content only in JavaScript, Google and AI engines will not read it.

Fix  Render the map's content in real, accessible HTML with proper text alternatives.
